# JourneyMap · Alpha guide (for a fundraising lead)

**What this is:** JourneyMap is a single file. Open it in **Chrome or Edge**
(not Firefox — Firefox does not reliably keep local data for files opened
from disk). Donor data never leaves your computer; nothing is uploaded.

**Session one (≈10 minutes)**
1. **Templates ▾ → Annual giving** — a suggested map of the annual fund journey: stage columns (left→right), lanes (experience / touchpoints / org actions / gaps), and a recurring-episodes lane (the appeal calendar).
2. **Rename the stages** to your own words — click a stage title and type.
3. **Edit a card** — click any card: change the title/note, mark it **💡 Idea**, set a metric.
4. **Drag a stage column** to reorder it; **drag a card** between cells; press **C**, click card → card, to draw a flow arrow.
5. **Set one metric the way you would**: open a card → ＋ Metric → name it, current number, suggested benchmark (verify it!), goal.
6. **Export ▾ → PNG image** — the board-ready map.

**The ground rules**
- **Saved automatically.** You'll see "✓ Saved" in the bar after any change. There is no Save button to hunt for.
- **One machine at a time.** Your maps live in this browser. Moving to another computer: **More ▾ → Download a copy…**, then on the other machine **Open a saved copy…**.
- **Undo anything:** Undo / Redo in the toolbar, or the "Undo" button on deletion toasts.
- **Printing to PDF:** Export ▾ → PDF (print), then in the print dialog choose "Save as PDF" and turn off headers/footers if your browser shows them.
