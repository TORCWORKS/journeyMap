Using JourneyMap

  • Stages — the columns of the journey, left to right. Rename by clicking, delete with the ×, add stages with + Stage or the dashed column at the end.
  • Lanes — each row is a layer: what donors experience, the channels they use, what your org does, and gaps & opportunities. Toggle lanes in the bar above.
  • Cards — click any cell to add one; click a card's ⋯ to edit it. Drag cards between cells to move them.
  • Connect mode — press Connect, then click two cards to draw a flow line. Click again to remove it.
  • Undo — Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z. Your map autosaves in this browser.
  • Export — Export PNG makes a clean image for decks; PDF prints the whole map. Save file keeps a copy to reload later.
